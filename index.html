Why Bodakdev Treats KOMO Like a Second Living Room.

Conceived by the team behind Kaffa Coffee Roasters, KOMO was built to answer a simple question: What if a cafe felt like a sculpted earthen gallery, yet served comfort food bold enough for an Amdavadi palate at midnight?
