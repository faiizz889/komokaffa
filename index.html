OUR STORY & ARCHITECTURE

Where Earthen Tranquility Meets Lively Gastronomy

Born from Ahmedabad's celebrated coffee pioneers, KOMO by KAFFA was envisioned as a tactile departure from ordinary dining. Inspired by carved sandstone grottos and wabi-sabi minimalism, every wall curves organically—textured in warm sand stucco, adorned with hand-embossed tree branches, and bathed in golden recessed cove light.

Whether you gather around our grand communal timber table with plush linen-tied chairs or tuck into an intimate window alcove framed by glowing ceramics, our kitchen pairs wood-fired Italian bistro classics and pan-Asian dimsum with Kaffa's legendary specialty coffee.

Artisanal Craft

Hand-stretched sourdough pizzas, house-folded dimsum, and single-origin roasts.

Warm Hospitality

Attentive, professional table service dedicated to unhurried dining until 1 AM.
